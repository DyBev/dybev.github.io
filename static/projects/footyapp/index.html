Footyapp

A fantasy football app built for a fantasy league that my friend was running, they wanted to detach themselves from the FPL format and have a bit more interaction between the league members

Built on a core concept of a "Auction" where the entire league gets together and then place bids on each player where the league manager leading the auction

Each player get's a total of of "300 million" to spend on bidding for players and a total of 15 players per team

The league bidding happens 2/3 times per season depending on how the league was run sometimes the league members spent all of their cash in the first 2 auctions so the 3rd is skipped

If members wanted to buy new players they would need to forefit a previous player to buy the new one, but of course there is no gurantee, since other players also have the opportunity to bid, as you can imagine there were a few late season upsets

Sadly this is one of those projects that outgrew me, legacy decisions combined with me still learning and growing as an engineer, some decisions I made made it difficult to continue itterating and improving the core app

I also ended getting bitten by the changes in the API provider, they made a change that meant that seasons past the 2024/2025 season were not available on the free plan
